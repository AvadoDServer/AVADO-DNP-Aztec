Become an Aztec sequencer

A sequencer builds and votes on Aztec blocks and earns AZTEC. You need 200,000 AZTEC, staked from your own wallet.

  1. Make the keys and write the 24 words on paper.
  2. Send 0.01 ETH to the sequencer address, for Ethereum fees.
  3. Stake on stake.aztec.network with the staking file.
  4. Wait in the queue until this page says “Active”.

Reward address (optional)
Before you start
  • Your stake, and the right to take it back, stay with your own wallet on the Aztec staking website. The box never sees it.
  • The box keeps the sequencer's own keys: an Ethereum key that signs and pays the small Ethereum fees, and a second key for voting.
  • If the box is off for long, the sequencer misses its turns and can lose part of the stake as a penalty. Keep the box online.

To stake: download the staking file (it appears after step 1), open stake.aztec.network, connect the wallet that holds your AZTEC, choose “Stake”, then “Run your own Sequencer”, upload the file and confirm in your wallet.

Less than 200,000 AZTEC? You can delegate it to a staking provider instead; see Aztec's staking guide.

Remove the sequencer keys

The node stops being a sequencer. If you are staked, you then miss your turns and can be penalised: first take your stake out on stake.aztec.network. You can make the same keys again later from your 24 words. Type REMOVE to confirm.

More options

For the best connection to other sequencers, let port 40400 (TCP and UDP) through your router to this AVADO. It also works without, with fewer connections.
